Questions

Common questions

How long does the early-adopter price last?

The early-adopter offer runs until 31 March 2027, or when the first 50 companies have joined, whichever comes first. Sign up while it is open and your price is locked in for 3 years from the date you joined. It is not a contract and you are free to leave.

Is there a contract?

No. There is no minimum term and no minimum number of users. You can cancel from the billing settings at any time, and access continues to the end of the period you have paid for.

How do I join?

Book a demo. Keiran walks through Compliar on a demo account and sets your company up himself.

What happens to my price after three years?

The three-year lock means the prices you joined at cannot go up for three years. After that, any change to the published prices applies with notice, as set out in the terms, and you can still leave at any time.

Can I add or remove users during the lock?

Yes. Users are mixed and changed as your team changes. Added users are charged pro rata and keep your locked price per plan.

What does it cost?

Lite is £35, Office £65 and Design £95 per user per month plus VAT, or ten months for the year if you pay yearly. A Lite plan paid yearly works out at about £1 a day.
