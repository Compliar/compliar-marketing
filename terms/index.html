3A. Site log book, stickers and onboarding

Site log book. The site log book costs £4 plus VAT per site per year. A contractor may add it to a customer's service plan at their own price; if it is added part-way through the year, it is charged to the customer straight away. A site activated by scanning a QR sticker has 7 days to pay the log book amount, and we send reminders 2 weeks before and 1 day before the payment date.

When cover lapses. If log book cover is not renewed, the log book becomes read-only 7 days after the payment date. Records stay visible and nothing is deleted, but no new entries can be added until it is renewed.

Log-book-only accounts. A log-book-only account has no per-user plan. It is for UK sites only, works to British Standards and is available to any business that holds several properties, such as estate agents, housing providers and property developers. The rest of Compliar is for electrical, fire and security contractors.

QR sticker orders. Printed stickers (named-site, blank QR and false alarm notice) cost £1 plus VAT each, plus £3.95 plus VAT postage per order. We deliver to UK addresses only. The stickers belong to the company that buys them. The £4 a year log book charge is separate and starts when a sticker is activated onto a site. You can also print stickers yourself at no charge.

Onboarding. Onboarding is optional. It starts from £195 plus VAT and is quoted individually. A self-service route is available to everyone after a demo.

3B. Subcontractor access

A subcontractor is a separate business that takes work from you job by job. A subcontractor can sign in to Compliar to see and update only the jobs you give them.

Anyone who is employed by you, is paid through your PAYE, or works regularly under your own management is not a subcontractor for the purposes of Compliar. They need a paid user.

Each subcontractor sign-in costs £15 plus VAT per month. We charge it only while that subcontractor has at least one live job from you (offered or accepted), and the charge follows the jobs as they start and finish. A subcontractor with no live job costs nothing and sees nothing. Subcontractor access is free during a free trial.

We may look at how subcontractor access is being used on your account. If a sign-in looks like it belongs to someone who is employed by you, or who works for you continuously rather than job by job, we will tell you first. We may then move that person onto a paid user from the next billing date.

You can end a subcontractor’s access at any time by setting an end date on their record.
